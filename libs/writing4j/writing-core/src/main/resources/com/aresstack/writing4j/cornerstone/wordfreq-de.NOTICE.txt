wordfreq-de.txt.gz: German word frequencies of the general language

Derived from the German word list "large_de" of wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437).
Change: converted from MessagePack to text; one line per frequency bucket of one centibel
("<bucket>\t<word> <word> ..."; Zipf value = 9 - bucket / 100); words below Zipf 1.5 left out.
